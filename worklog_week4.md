# CIA Research Activity

**Week:** 4
**Day:** 1
**Topic:** Statistics
**Activity:** Research – Statistical Concepts Frequently Tested in Data Analyst Interviews

### 1. Objective

To identify the most frequently tested statistical concepts in Data Analyst interviews and understand their practical applications in real-world business scenarios.

### 2. Research Query

"What statistical concepts do data analyst interviews test most frequently?"

### 3. Key Learnings

Through CIA research, I identified the following 10 important statistical concepts:

1. **Descriptive Statistics:** Mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation and percentiles.
2. **Probability Distributions:** Normal, Binomial, Poisson and Exponential distributions.
3. **Hypothesis Testing:** T-test, Chi-square test, ANOVA, p-values and confidence intervals.
4. **Correlation and Causation:** Pearson correlation, Spearman correlation and covariance.
5. **Sampling Techniques:** Random, stratified, cluster and systematic sampling.
6. **Regression Analysis:** Linear regression, coefficients, R-squared and residual analysis.
7. **Time-Series Analysis:** Trend, seasonality, moving averages and ARIMA.
8. **Statistical Errors:** Type I error, Type II error and statistical power.
9. **Non-Parametric Tests:** Mann-Whitney U test and Kruskal-Wallis test.
10. **Bayesian Statistics:** Prior probability, posterior probability and Bayes' theorem.

### 4. Practical Applications in Indian Companies

* Retail companies use descriptive statistics to analyse sales and customer purchasing behaviour.
* E-commerce companies use probability and hypothesis testing for business experiments.
* Banking companies use regression and statistical models for risk analysis.
* IT companies use sampling techniques to analyse large datasets efficiently.
* Businesses use time-series analysis to forecast future sales and demand.

### 5. Important Interview Learnings

I learned that:

* Median is more suitable than mean when data contains extreme values or outliers.
* A p-value helps determine whether observed results are statistically significant under the null hypothesis.
* Correlation does not necessarily imply causation.
* A confidence interval provides a range of plausible values for a population parameter.
* Box plots help identify data distribution and potential outliers.

### 6. Mini Mock Interview Preparation

I also explored common interview questions:

1. What is the difference between mean, median and mode?
2. What does a 95% confidence interval represent?
3. What is the difference between one-tailed and two-tailed tests?
4. Why does correlation not imply causation?
5. When should we use a Chi-square test?
6. What is the difference between Type I and Type II errors?

### 7. Conclusion

This CIA research activity improved my understanding of the statistical concepts required for Data Analyst roles. I learned how statistical techniques are applied in business decision-making, sales analysis, forecasting and experimentation.

I will focus on strengthening these concepts using Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, SciPy and Statsmodels and practise explaining statistical results through real-world datasets.
