# Create 10 KPIs
| No. | KPI                        | Formula                                             | Type    | Frequency |
| --- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------- | --------- |
| 1   | Average Stock Return       | Average of individual stock returns                 | Lagging | Daily     |
| 2   | Annualized Return          | Annualized percentage return                        | Lagging | Monthly   |
| 3   | Market Volatility          | Standard deviation of returns                       | Lagging | Daily     |
| 4   | Maximum Drawdown           | (Peak - Trough) / Peak × 100                        | Lagging | Monthly   |
| 5   | Trading Volume Growth      | (Current - Previous Volume) / Previous Volume × 100 | Leading | Daily     |
| 6   | Positive Stock Percentage  | Positive stocks / Total stocks × 100                | Leading | Daily     |
| 7   | Negative Stock Percentage  | Negative stocks / Total stocks × 100                | Leading | Daily     |
| 8   | Sector Performance         | Average return within a sector                      | Lagging | Monthly   |
| 9   | Risk-Adjusted Return       | Portfolio Return / Portfolio Volatility             | Lagging | Monthly   |
| 10  | Market Recovery Percentage | Current Value / Previous Peak × 100                 | Leading | Monthly   |

## Introduction

The main objective of this KPI framework is to understand how the Indian stock market is performing and identify important changes in stock returns, trading activity and market risk.

I selected 10 key performance indicators covering market performance, sector movements, trading volume and investment risk. Each KPI has a defined calculation method, responsible owner, monitoring frequency and target.

I also classified the KPIs into leading and lagging indicators to understand both potential market movements and actual performance results.

These indicators will help present complex stock market information in a simpler and more meaningful way through a Power BI dashboard.
