# AutoViz vs SweetViz – Automated EDA Comparison

## AutoViz
AutoViz automatically generates multiple visualizations, including distribution plots, violin plots, heatmaps and categorical analysis. It is useful for quickly exploring datasets and identifying patterns.

## SweetViz
SweetViz generates detailed and interactive HTML reports containing dataset summaries, variable distributions, correlations and feature associations. Its reports are easy to understand and suitable for presenting findings.

## Comparison
AutoViz is useful for quickly generating multiple visualizations with minimal configuration. SweetViz provides a more structured and interactive HTML report with detailed information about individual variables and their relationships. Both tools reduce manual effort in Exploratory Data Analysis. AutoViz is suitable for analysts who want quick visual exploration, whereas SweetViz is more convenient for presenting organized analysis to non-technical stakeholders.

## Business Stakeholder Recommendation

I recommend SweetViz for business stakeholders because it generates structured, interactive HTML reports that make dataset characteristics, distributions, missing values and relationships easier to understand. Its presentation is suitable for sharing analytical findings with management teams.
